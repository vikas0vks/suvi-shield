# Third-party notices

Production rule builds compile data from maintained filter projects. Their data remains under its original license and attribution requirements.

- EasyList, EasyPrivacy, regional lists, cookie notices and Fanboy lists: https://easylist.to/ (GPL-3.0-or-later, CC-BY-SA-3.0 or CC-BY-3.0 as recorded per source).
- uBlock Origin uAssets lists: https://github.com/uBlockOrigin/uAssets (GPL-3.0-or-later).
- AdGuard filters: https://github.com/AdguardTeam/AdguardFilters (GPL-3.0-or-later).
- Online Malicious URL Blocklist: https://gitlab.com/malware-filter/urlhaus-filter (CC0-1.0).
- OISD: https://oisd.nl/ (GPL-3.0).
- Peter Lowe's server list: https://pgl.yoyo.org/adservers/ (McRae GPL with non-commercial terms).
- 1Hosts Lite: https://github.com/badmojr/1Hosts (see the source repository's terms and credits).
- ABPindo: https://github.com/ABPindo/indonesianadblockrules (GPL-3.0-or-later and CC-BY-SA-3.0).
- RU AdList: https://github.com/easylist/ruadlist (CC-BY-3.0).

The exact source URLs, titles, hashes, byte sizes and fallback state for a generated build are recorded in `src/generated/rules-report.json`.
